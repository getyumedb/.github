# YumeDB

**The open knowledge graph of anime and manga.**

Anime, manga, characters, people and studios connected in a single graph, with personal tracking, an airing schedule and the Tracker, a browser extension that logs what you watch on streaming platforms.

## What it does

- **Catalog**: anime, manga, characters, people and studios, with genres, an airing schedule, your library, your progress and a public profile.
- **The Tracker**: a browser extension that recognizes the show and episode you're watching on Crunchyroll, HIDIVE, Netflix or Bilibili and marks it as watched in one click.
- **Always in sync**: the extension saves locally first, so it works offline, and syncs both ways with device tokens you can revoke.
- **A catalog that keeps up**: data from several open sources is merged into one canonical entry per work and refreshed while shows are airing.

## Status

YumeDB **v2** is being rebuilt from scratch. Our repositories are private for now.
